Federal Reserve Holds Rates Amid Persistent Core Inflation
The Federal Open Market Committee voted 9–3 to hold the federal funds rate at 4.25–4.50% at its June 2026 meeting. Three members dissented in favour of an immediate 25bps cut, citing weakening labour market data. Core PCE remains at 2.4%, above the 2% target but on a sustained downward trajectory.
